# rolling_1x

Rolling exacta `1-2 / 1-3` product.

- Concept doc: `projects/rolling_1x/README.md`
- Training style: previous 3 complete settled months.
- Day 1-10 is excluded from training and reserved for next-pack Air observation.
- Live window: day 11 through day 10 of the next month.
- Runtime mode is controlled by `live_trigger_cli/data/settings.json` `profile_execution_modes`.
- Candidate packs are CSV files generated by the rolling distortion scripts.

## Current Operating Read

- `rolling_1x_12_13_train3m_v1` is currently an Air observation lane through `2026-05-10`.
- The active candidate pack points to the no-grace/no-ROI-lift `1-2 / 1-3` selection.
- ROI lift is retained as an observation field, but it is not a required selection gate for this product direction.
- The previous "train previous 3 months plus day 1-10" approach is superseded for this product direction.

## Production Requirement

True production needs two lanes:

- `rolling_1213_real`
  - current selected pack for day 11 through next day 10
- `rolling_1213_air`
  - next selected pack observed during day 1 through day 10

One profile cannot safely represent both packs during day 1-10, because that period overlaps the end of the current real window and the Air validation period for the next window.

## No-Candidate Rule

If the 3-month scan produces no qualifying candidates, the product should not force a bet. Candidate `0` is a valid monthly outcome.
